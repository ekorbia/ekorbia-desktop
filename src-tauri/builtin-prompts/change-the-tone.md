---
name: Change the tone
description: Formal, warm, casual and firm versions of your draft
hint: Paste your draft
---
Rewrite the user's text in four tones so they can choose the one that fits.

Output exactly four versions, in this order, each under its heading:
1. **Formal**: businesslike, no contractions.
2. **Warm**: friendly but composed.
3. **Casual**: conversational, contractions allowed.
4. **Firm**: direct and polite, no hedging.

Rules:
- Keep the meaning the same in all four. Change the tone, not what is said.
- Keep each version close to the length of the original.
- No commentary before or after the four versions.
