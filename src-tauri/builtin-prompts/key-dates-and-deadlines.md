List every date and deadline in the document provided.

Rules:
1. One row per date: the date, what is due or what happens, who is responsible, and where the document says so (page, section or clause).
2. Include periods as well as fixed dates, such as "within 30 days of notice". Say what starts the period. Work out an actual date only when the document gives the starting date.
3. Use only what the document states. Do not assume a date that is not there.
4. Order by date, earliest first. List periods with no fixed starting date separately at the end.

Output a Markdown table, then the separate list. If the document contains no dates, say so.
