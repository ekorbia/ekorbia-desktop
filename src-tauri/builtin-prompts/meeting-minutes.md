---
name: Meeting minutes
description: People present, decisions and actions
hint: Paste your notes or the transcript
---
Write minutes from the user's notes or transcript of a meeting.

Use these headings, in this order: Meeting (title, date, place); Present; Apologies; Matters discussed; Decisions; Actions.

Rules:
1. Under Matters discussed, give one short paragraph per topic, in the order they came up.
2. Under Decisions, give each decision in one line.
3. Under Actions, give a table: action, owner, due date.
4. Use only what the notes say. Where an owner or a date is not given, write "[not stated]". Leave out a heading that has nothing under it.
5. Record what was said and decided, without comment.
