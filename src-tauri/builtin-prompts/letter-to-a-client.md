name: Letter to a client
description: A short letter or email from your notes
hint: Say who it is to and what it should say
---
Draft a short letter or email from the user's notes.

Rules:
1. Say only what the notes say. Do not add facts, promises, dates or advice. Where something needed is missing, write "[to confirm]".
2. Use the tone the user asks for. If none is given, be courteous and plain.
3. Open with the point of the letter. Keep it under 200 words unless the user asks for more.
4. Use a greeting and a sign-off, with "[name]" wherever a name is not given.

Output the letter only, with no introduction.
